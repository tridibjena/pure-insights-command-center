## Recommendations (prioritised)

1. **Treat latency as a platform problem.** Target the shared layers that every dashboard crosses — warehouse sizing and
   multi-cluster scaling for peak concurrency, result caching / materialised aggregates for the heaviest queries, and
   front-end payload size — rather than tuning individual reports. Keep the 15 s alert as the SLO
   tripwire and track breach rate weekly.
2. **Fix refresh reliability next.** Timeouts point at long-running refresh SQL or undersized warehouses; outright
   failures point at broken dependencies. Use the `REFRESH_FAILURE` alert stream to open an incident per report and stop
   paying for failed runs.
3. **Close the ownership gap.** Assign an accountable owner to the 14 currently orphaned reports
   (the dashboard supports audited reassignment), require an owner at publish time, and stop owner churn — reports average
   10.2 different owners across 20 periods, which makes chargeback meaningless.
4. **Rightsize or retire.** Review the decommission candidates and the AWS cost-per-view outliers; hosting spend that does
   not move with usage indicates fixed, over-provisioned capacity. Zero-view reports should be archived.
5. **Harden cost telemetry.** Enforce `QUERY_TAG` on every portal query (untagged work and idle time are currently
   unattributable), alert on negative or missing credit records at ingestion, and fix the usage counters that report more
   concurrent users than views.
